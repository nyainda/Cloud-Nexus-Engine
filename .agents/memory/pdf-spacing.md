---
name: PDF page spacing
description: User preference for compact, well-used invoice, quotation, and debt PDF layouts
---

Keep customer-facing PDFs compact and make deliberate use of page space. Preserve readable, full-width item and financial tables; do not reserve a large side column for decorative stamps. Place seals at the lower right without overlapping content or the footer, and add continuation pages only when content requires them.

**Why:** The user has repeatedly asked that invoice, quotation, and debt PDFs not waste space or distort their tables.

**How to apply:** When changing a PDF, render both a short and multi-page example, then adjust vertical placement and seal size before reducing type size, table width, or row readability.
